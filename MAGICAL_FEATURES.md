## 🩺 Medication Management

**Life-critical feature for users managing multiple medications**

### What It Does
- Voice-based medication scheduling ("I take aspirin, one tablet, at 9am and 9pm")
- Automatic reminder calls via Twilio
- Medication adherence tracking
- Family notifications for missed doses
- Simple, conversational interface

### How It Works
**User:** "I need to remember to take my blood pressure tablets"
**Serenity:** "Of course! Tell me the name of your medication, how much you take, and what times you need to take it."
**User:** "It's called Ramipril, one tablet, at 9 in the morning"
**Serenity:** "I've added Ramipril, one tablet, to your medication schedule. I'll remind you daily at 9 in the morning."

### Voice Commands
- "Add medication [name]"
- "What medications am I taking?"
- "I just took my [medication name]"
- "What's my next medication?"
- "How am I doing with my medications?" (adherence stats)

### Example Conversation
```
User: "What medications do I need to take?"
Serenity: "You're currently taking 2 medications:
           1. Aspirin, one tablet, at 9 in the morning.
           2. Blood pressure tablets, one tablet, at 9 in the morning and 9 in the evening.
           Would you like me to add another one?"
```

### Impact
- **Prevents hospitalizations** - Medication non-adherence causes 125,000 deaths/year in US
- **Peace of mind** - Never forget important medications
- **Family reassurance** - Caregivers notified if doses are missed

## 🆘 Emergency SOS System

**Voice-activated emergency alerts for safety and peace of mind**

### What It Does
- Quick access to family contacts ("Call my daughter!")
- Emergency protocol triggered by voice ("Help!" or "Emergency!")
- Automatic escalation (try family first, then 999)
- Daily wellness check-ins
- Welfare check alerts if user doesn't check in

### How It Works
**Setting up contacts:**
**User:** "I want to add an emergency contact"
**Serenity:** "Of course. What's their name and phone number?"
**User:** "My daughter Mary, 07411 234 567"
**Serenity:** "I've added Mary, your daughter, as emergency contact number 1. If you ever need help, just say 'Help' or 'Emergency' and I'll call Mary right away."

**In an emergency:**
**User:** "Help! I've fallen!"
**Serenity:** "I'm calling Mary right now. Help is on the way. Stay calm, you're going to be okay."
[Automatically calls Mary's phone]
[Sends SMS: "Emergency alert from [phone number]. They requested help. Please check on them immediately."]

### Voice Commands
- "Help!" or "Emergency!" - Triggers emergency protocol
- "Add emergency contact" - Add family/neighbor contacts
- "Who are my emergency contacts?"
- "Call [contact name]"

### Safety Features
- **Automatic escalation**: If family doesn't answer, offers to connect to 999
- **Daily check-ins**: "Good morning! Just checking in - how are you today?"
- **Welfare alerts**: If user doesn't check in for 2+ days, family is notified
- **Fall detection support**: Ready to integrate with wearable sensors

### Impact
- **Saves lives** - Rapid response to medical emergencies
- **Reduces isolation** - Regular check-ins combat loneliness
- **Family peace of mind** - Know their loved one is safe

## 💚 Daily Companionship

**Combat loneliness with warm, personalized daily routines**

### What It Does
- Personalized morning greetings
- Gentle evening wind-down conversations
- Birthday and special occasion remembrance
- Mood tracking with emotional support
- Conversation continuity (remembers what you talked about)
- Gentle encouragement and positivity

### Morning Greetings
**Example:**
```
"Good morning, Margaret! It's a lovely Thursday morning.
I hope you slept well. How are you feeling today?
Have you had your breakfast yet?"
```

**On birthdays:**
```
"Good morning, Margaret! And what a special morning it is - it's your birthday!
Many happy returns, my dear. I hope you have a wonderful day filled with joy
and celebration. Have any family members called yet to wish you well?"
```

### Evening Check-ins
**Example:**
```
"Good evening, Margaret. I hope you've had a pleasant day.
Did you hear from your grandson Tom today?
Is there anything you'd like to chat about before you settle in for the evening?"
```

### Conversation Memory
The system remembers:
- User's preferred name
- Family members ("How's your daughter Mary?" "Did Tom visit?")
- Favorite topics ("Have you had time for any gardening recently?")
- Previous conversations ("How did that doctor's appointment go?")
- Important dates (birthdays, anniversaries)

### Emotional Support
**Detecting loneliness:**
**User:** "I've been feeling quite lonely today. Nobody's called."
**Serenity:** "I'm sorry you're feeling lonely. I'm here with you, and we can chat about anything you'd like. Have you thought about calling Mary? Or would you like to hear a cheerful story to lift your spirits?"

**Detecting sadness:**
**User:** "I'm feeling a bit down today."
**Serenity:** "I'm sorry you're not feeling your best. That's perfectly alright. Would you like to talk about what's bothering you? Sometimes it helps to share. Or would you prefer a distraction? I could tell you a cheerful story or share some good news from today."

### Mood Tracking
Automatically tracks mood over time and provides insights:
- "You've been sounding much happier this week - that's wonderful to hear!"
- "I've noticed you've mentioned feeling lonely a few times. Would you like me to help you connect with family more often?"

### Impact
- **Combats loneliness** - 1 in 4 UK seniors report feeling lonely most of the time
- **Mental health support** - Regular positive interactions improve wellbeing
- **Cognitive engagement** - Conversations keep the mind active
- **Sense of routine** - Predictable companionship creates comfort

## 💡 Design Philosophy

### Voice-First, Not Screen-First
- No apps to download
- No buttons to press
- No screens to read
- Just natural conversation

### Empathy Over Efficiency
- Take time for small talk
- Remember personal details
- Offer emotional support
- Never rush the user

### Safety Over Features
- Emergency access is always priority #1
- Medication tracking is never skipped
- Daily check-ins catch problems early
- Family involvement is encouraged

### Dignity & Independence
- Respect user autonomy
- Offer help, don't impose
- Celebrate small victories
- Maintain privacy (GDPR compliant)
